**What the change can and cannot be credited to.**

- **The O1 fix is credited with the rival ties only.** Matrix 2 lost 4 runs to rival ties
  (F-02 x3, F-03 x1). None recurs as a sigmoid tie in matrix 3. The offline rescore
  (`python -m eval.rescore`, BUILD_LOG section 7) recovers all 4 on their original,
  unchanged evidence. That is the measurement of the fix.
- **F-06 3/6 -> 6/6 is not a fix.** Matrix 2's F-06 misses were vague mechanism wording (O2)
  and one engine failure. Nothing was changed for either; the model happened to name the
  mechanism this time.
- **F-03 rep3 off was detected this time because of the load, not the fix** (O8, O9). Matrix 3
  ran on a different part of k6's 24-hour load curve and had no run at the trough.
- **F-04 and F-05 each lost a run.** Neither miss is caused by the fix. Both come from
  mechanisms that were already there (below).
- **n = 36.** At ~80% accuracy, one matrix's run-to-run noise is about ±2 runs, so +4 runs
  overall is suggestive, not conclusive. The per-fault F-02 / F-03 movement, together with
  the offline rescore, is the stronger evidence.
